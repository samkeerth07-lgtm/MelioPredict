# 🩺 MelioPredict – Machine Learning Based Melioidosis Disease Risk Prediction System

MelioPredict is a **Machine Learning-based healthcare web application** designed to predict the risk of **Melioidosis** using patient symptoms, medical history, and environmental exposure factors.

The system integrates a **trained Machine Learning classification model** with a modern **React frontend** and **FastAPI backend** to provide early disease risk assessment, confidence scores, and clinical guidance. It aims to support early screening and raise awareness about Melioidosis, particularly among individuals exposed to high-risk environmental conditions.

> ⚠️ **Disclaimer:** This application is intended for educational and screening purposes only. It is **not a substitute for professional medical diagnosis or treatment**.

---

# 🎯 Project Objective

The objective of this project is to develop an intelligent **Machine Learning-based prediction system** that assists in the early identification of Melioidosis risk by analyzing:

- Clinical Symptoms
- Medical History
- Environmental Exposure
- Patient Demographics

The application provides an instant prediction along with confidence scores, severity assessment, diagnostic test suggestions, and medical recommendations to support early clinical decision-making.

# 🧠 Machine Learning

This project uses a **supervised Machine Learning classification model** trained on clinical and environmental risk factors associated with Melioidosis.

### Input Features

- Age
- Gender
- Fever
- Weakness
- Skin Redness
- Skin Swelling
- Skin Ulcer
- Joint Pain
- Difficulty Moving
- Persistent Cough
- Diabetes
- Kidney Disease
- Lung Disease
- Immune Disorder
- Soil Contact
- Rain Exposure
- Open Wound
- Farming Activity
- Flood Water Exposure

### Model Output

The trained model predicts:

- Disease Risk (High Risk / Low Risk)
- Prediction Confidence
- Severity Level
- Possible Clinical Presentation

The prediction is further enhanced by generating clinical guidance and educational recommendations based on the patient's reported symptoms and risk factors.
